A Small Web Reverie

March Sixth. Twenty Twenty Five.

Cameron Thomas

I've been seeing a lot of small web revival stuff lately, though its obvious to me that it doesnt have a mainstream grip yet. Still, its been lingering on my mind for the past few weeks. I've been thinking about this website, sitting out there. Stagnant. How I make a plan to revise it and modernize it every few years. I've repaved it over with React, and Vue, and React again. and again. and again. Now, one bored day, i've been working on re-writing it minimalistic-like.

I uninstalled almost all social media from my phone recently, and i feel as though im seeing the world in a different light. I didnt realize how angry it was making me. And it shoved this whole small-web movement into the center of my noggin. How cool would it be to connect with folks on an individual level online again. To see what they've been creating, and how they express themselves. Like the clothes they wear, their website design speaks to who they as a human. Like MySpace way back when.

Idk, in lieu of waxing about the movement, ill say it seems like a fun way to kill some time. And it may be a nice way for me to journal. Publicly too. I hope to share what i've been working on here, via a blog-like system. Probably all static web pages.

Tenants

There's a few tenants i have for web design that have been influencing this website

Link Rot

Everything I share on here, should be permanent. Always and forever, if you get a link from this site, it should resolve. If it doesnt. Please email me at cameron.w.thomas@gmail.com and call me a fucking loser who doesnt stand by his principals.

Minimal Loading Times

If it can be referenced localy. It should be. I thought about storing these blog entries in a database. But then it hit me that every time you pull up this page, it will have to pull up another resource from elsewhere. It feels wrong to do it this way, but maybe the old guard had a point (even if they themselves didnt know it).

Minimal Dependencies

This whole thing should be tiny. As small as possible while still being a cool webpage. So if I want a cool javascript package or something, you can bet I'll try to recreate it myself first.

Creativity Trumps Most

Aside from link-rot if I have a really cool idea thats gonna require loading times or extra dependencies, I reserve the right to ignore all my other tenants to execute that. Just so long as the core of the website runs nice and smooth.

Connection

I dont know how these small web pages reach out to eachother. I assume there's some sort of hub or inter-linkage between them. Should i find this info, rest assured I'll share it here.

That's all for now. Hope I keep up with this.
